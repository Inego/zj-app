# Standard Zung Jung — Miscellaneous Table Rules

Source: https://www.zj-mahjong.info/book_en/1.1%20Misc%20Rules.html  
Chinese: book_hk/1.1 Misc Rules.html (v3.2 text; compatible with v3.3 scoring)

Recommended standard rules. First three strongly recommended; others may vary for house rules.

## 1. Winning hand definition

- **Regular hand:** 4 sets (sequence / triplet / kong) + 1 pair (eyes).
- **Irregular:** only those listed in category 10.
- Categories 1–9 are **scoring patterns only**, not alternate win shapes.
  - e.g. All Honors may win only as completed regular hand or Seven Pairs.

## 2. Freedom of Count (自由數和)

- If concealed tiles admit multiple set arrangements, winner freely chooses **one** arrangement and scores that arrangement only.
- Cannot combine patterns from different arrangements.
  - Forbidden examples: Three Identical Sequences **and** Three Consecutive Triplets; Two Identical Sequences Twice **and** Seven Pairs.
- Details + examples: `freedom-of-count.md`.

## 3. Concealed triplet (for series 4.2)

- Concealed: all three from wall (incl. self-draw win tile).
- Exposed: one claimed via pong or win-on-discard.

## 4. No sacred-discard prohibitions

- Same-round immunity **replaces** sacred discard / 過水 / furiten-like bans.
- May win whenever hand is complete.
- No similar bans on pong/kong claims.

## 5. Passing the deal

- Deal passes after **every** hand.
- East **never** repeats (no renchan). Controls session length.

## 6. Dead wall

- Dead wall = **14** tiles unused.
- 15th-last wall tile = **seabed** (last drawable).
- After seabed player discards and no win → **draw / no-win** (流局).

## 7. Interception (截和)

- Classical interception on competing win claims.
- Precedence among win claims: discarder's lower seat > opposite > upper seat.
- Claim priority overall: win > pong/kong > chi.

## 8. Immediate disclosure of concealed kong

- Declare concealed kong → reveal all four tiles immediately for verification.

## 9. Thirteen Terminals — no privileges

- Cannot rob a **concealed** kong.
- Can be intercepted by any other hand.

## 10. Win-on-kong vs robbing kong (payoff classification)

| Event | Classification |
|---|---|
| Win on Kong (嶺上開花) | **Self-draw** |
| Robbing a Kong (搶槓) | **Win on discard** |

Affects responsibility split for big hands.

## Not used in standard ZJ

- Prevailing wind / double wind.
- Sacred discard / furiten-style restrictions.
- Minimum points (unless official 5-pt variant).
- Basic points / 副底 (except chicken = 1, or casual variants).
- Self-draw score inflation.
