# Basic Mahjong Rules (play layer)

Source: https://www.zj-mahjong.info/book_en/App%20C%20-%20Mahjong%20Rules.html  
Purpose: tile play only (not full shuffle/deal ritual). Scoring = separate ZJ docs.

## Tile set

- **136 tiles**, **34 designs** × 4.
- Suits (number 1–9): Bamboos (索), Characters (萬), Dots (筒) → 27 designs.
- Honors: 4 Winds (ESWN) + 3 Dragons (White/Green/Red) → 7 designs.
- Optional: bonus/flower tiles, jokers (not standard competition).

## Objective

- Start: each player **13** tiles (East may have 14 after first deal draw depending on local deal ritual; play aims to complete 14-tile win).
- First to complete a **winning hand**.

## Winning shapes

### Regular hand

- 4 sets + 1 pair (eyes).
- Set types:
  - **Sequence (chow):** 3 consecutive numbers, same suit. Honors cannot sequence.
  - **Triplet (pong set):** 3 identical.
  - **Kong:** 4 identical, must be **declared**; draw supplement to keep hand size correct.

### Irregular hands

- Defined by scoring system (ZJ: Thirteen Terminals, Seven Pairs).

## Draw and discard

- On turn: draw from wall → discard one.
- May claim fresh discards (not past discards).

## Claims

### Win

- **Calling / ready:** one tile short of complete hand.
- **Self-draw:** draw completing tile.
- **Win on discard:** claim opponent discard; discarder = discarder/responsible.

### Pong

- Hold pair; claim third identical from any player.
- Melds face-up, locked. Then discard; turn to lower seat.

### Chi

- Hold 2/3 of a sequence; claim missing tile **only from upper seat**.
- Melds face-up, locked. Then discard; turn to lower seat.

### Kong types

| Type | How |
|---|---|
| Concealed kong | Four in hand; declare |
| Small exposed kong | Add 4th from hand to existing exposed pong |
| Big exposed kong | Hold concealed triplet; claim 4th from discard |

- After kong: draw **supplement**, then discard.
- Kong locked; cannot rearrange.

### Robbing a kong

- When opponent declares **small exposed kong**, and that tile completes your win → claim for win.
- Concealed kong **cannot** be robbed (ZJ: Thirteen Terminals also cannot rob concealed kong).

## Claim precedence

1. Win (highest)
2. Pong / big exposed kong
3. Chi (lowest)

Multiple win claims → **interception** by seat order (lower > opposite > upper of discarder). Some modern rules allow multi-win; ZJ uses classical interception.

## Dead wall / draw game

- ZJ: last **14** tiles unused; 15th-last is seabed.
- Exhaust live wall without win → no-win.

## Scoring vs payoff (terms)

- **Scoring system:** evaluate hand content → point value.
- **Payoff scheme:** how non-winners split payment to winner.
- ZJ separates these cleanly; fixed-income principle on scoring side.
