# Kong

A **kong** is four identical tiles, **declared** as a set. After any
kong declaration you draw a **supplement** tile (from the wall end used
for supplements), then usually discard — unless that supplement wins
the hand ([Win on Kong (9.2)](pattern-9.2)).

## Concealed kong

You hold all four in hand and declare. In Zung Jung the four tiles are
revealed for verification. A concealed kong does **not** break
[Concealed Hand (1.2)](pattern-1.2).

A concealed kong **cannot** be robbed.

## Big exposed kong

You hold a **concealed** triplet and claim the fourth tile from
someone's discard. The kong is open.

## Small exposed kong

You already have an **open pung** and add the fourth tile from your
hand (often after drawing it). Promoting the pung to a kong is the
moment others may [rob the kong](rules-winning) if that tile completes
their win.

## After declaring

- The kong is locked; you cannot rearrange it.
- Draw the **supplement**, then discard (or win on the supplement).
- Kong counts as a set toward a [regular hand](rules-hands) and toward
  [kong patterns (4.3)](pattern-4.3).
