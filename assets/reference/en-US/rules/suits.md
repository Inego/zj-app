# Suits

A **suit** is one of the three numbered tile families. Each suit has
ranks **1–9** (nine designs × four copies). Together they make
**27** of the **34** designs in a standard set.

Only suited tiles form **[sequences](rules-sequence)** (three consecutive
ranks in the **same** suit). **[Honors](rules-honors)** — [winds](rules-winds)
and [dragons](rules-dragons) — are **not** suited and cannot sequence; see
[Tiles](rules-tiles).

Scoring often cares about how many suits appear:

- **One suit** — [One-Suit (2)](pattern-2) patterns (mixed or pure)
- **Same suit, same numbers** — [identical sequences (5)](pattern-5)
- **All three suits** — [similar sets (6)](pattern-6)
- **Same suit, consecutive ranks** — [consecutive sets (7)](pattern-7)
