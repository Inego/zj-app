# Overview

Mahjong is played around the world under several major rule families —
Hong Kong, Chinese competition (MCR), Taiwanese, American, and others.
Among them, **Japanese Riichi** is especially popular internationally
(online clients, clubs, and media). You may already know Riichi; this
app teaches a **different** ruleset.

## Zung Jung

**Zung Jung** (中庸, “Middle Way”) is a **pattern-based additive**
scoring system by **Alan Kwan** (關兆豪) of Hong Kong. It aims at a
clear competitive middle path between very sparse and very bloated
scoring lists. This reference follows **v3.3**.

Zung Jung was the scoring framework of the **World Series of Mahjong
(WSoM)**. It was used at three past WSoM competitions — **2008, 2010,
and 2015**.
