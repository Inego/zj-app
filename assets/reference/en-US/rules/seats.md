# Seats & winds

Four players sit as **East, South, West, North** in turn order. Your
**seat wind** is the wind of your current seat. The four wind
**tiles** are listed under [Winds](rules-winds).

## Seat wind

A triplet or kong of **your seat wind** is a
[Value Honor (3.1)](pattern-3.1) (+10 per such set), the same as any
[dragon](rules-dragons) triplet/kong.

Example: if you are West, {WWW} scores Value Honor for you; {EEE} does
not (unless you are East).

## No prevailing wind

Standard Zung Jung does **not** use a **prevailing wind** (round wind /
table wind). Only **seat wind** and **[dragons](rules-dragons)** count
as value honors. There is no “double wind” bonus from matching seat and
table wind.

## East and the deal

East is the starting dealer for the hand. Special opening wins:

- [Blessing of Heaven (9.4.1)](pattern-9.4.1) — East wins on the initial
  14-tile dealt hand
- [Blessing of Earth (9.4.2)](pattern-9.4.2) — a non-East player is
  calling on the initial 13 and wins on East's **first** discard

In standard Zung Jung table rules, the deal **passes every hand** (East
does not keep the deal after winning).
