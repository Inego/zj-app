# Eyes (pair)

Two **identical** tiles that complete a regular hand (the “pair” /
“eyes”).

:::center
{55s} · {WW}
:::

Eyes are **not** a set. You need exactly one pair in a
[regular hand](rules-hands) (unless you win with an irregular shape such
as [Seven Pairs](pattern-10.2)).

Eyes may be any design — numbers, [winds](rules-winds), or
[dragons](rules-dragons). Scoring patterns
sometimes care what the eyes are (for example honor eyes block
[No Terminals (1.3)](pattern-1.3)), but the hand shape itself only needs
one pair.
