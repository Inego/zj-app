# Honors

**Honors** are the seven non-suited designs: four **[winds](rules-winds)**
and three **[dragons](rules-dragons)**. They are not part of a numbered
[suit](rules-suits) and have **no rank order** for sequences.

:::center
{E}{S}{W}{N} · {P}{F}{C}
:::

## What honors can form

- [Triplets](rules-triplet) and [kongs](rules-kong-types) of the **same**
  design (three Easts, three Reds, …)
- [Eyes](rules-eyes) (a pair of the same design)

They **cannot** form [sequences](rules-sequence). One of each wind or one
of each dragon is not a set.

## Families

- **[Winds](rules-winds)** — East, South, West, North
- **[Dragons](rules-dragons)** — White, Green, Red
