# Concealed vs open

Tiles and sets are **concealed** or **open (exposed)** depending on how
they entered the hand.

## Concealed

Taken from the wall (including the tile that completes a **self-draw**
win). A fully [concealed hand](pattern-1.2) has no open melds before the
win; winning on a discard is still allowed for that pattern.

## Open (exposed)

A set completed by **claiming** another player's discard (chow, pung, or
big exposed kong), or the set completed when you **win on discard** on
that tile.

- Open melds sit face-up and are **locked** (you cannot rearrange them).
- For [concealed triplets (4.2)](pattern-4.2): if you win on discard of
  the completing tile, **that** triplet counts as **exposed**.

## Kongs

Kongs mix concealed and open rules — see [Kong](rules-kong-types). A
**concealed kong** does not break [Concealed Hand (1.2)](pattern-1.2);
an open kong does.
