# Dragons

Three **[honor](rules-honors)** designs — not a numbered [suit](rules-suits):

:::center
{P}{F}{C}
:::

- **White** (白) — {P}
- **Green** (發) — {F}
- **Red** (中) — {C}

Dragons have no rank order. They cannot form [sequences](rules-sequence)
— only [triplets](rules-triplet) or [kongs](rules-kong-types) of the
**same** design (three Reds, not “one of each dragon”).

Any dragon triplet or kong is always a
[Value Honor (3.1)](pattern-3.1) (+10 per set), for every player.
