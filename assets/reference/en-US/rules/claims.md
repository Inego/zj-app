# Claims

On another player's **fresh discard** (not an older tile in the river),
you may **claim** it if it completes a set or a win.

## Chow

- Complete a [sequence](rules-sequence) using the discarded tile.
- Only from the **upper seat** (the player who discards just before you —
  on your left in standard seating diagrams).
- After a chow: expose the meld, then discard; play continues from you.

## Pung

- Complete a [triplet](rules-triplet) using the discarded tile.
- From **any** player.
- After a pung: expose the meld, then discard; play continues from you
  (intervening players skip).

## Kong (from discard)

- **Big exposed kong:** hold a concealed triplet; claim the fourth from
  a discard. See [Kong](rules-kong-types).

## Win

- Claim the discard to complete a legal winning shape
  ([How you win](rules-winning)).
- Highest priority over set claims.

## Claim priority

When several players want the same discard:

1. **Win** (highest)
2. **Pung** or **big exposed kong**
3. **Chow** (lowest)

If several players claim a **win**, [interception](rules-winning)
resolves who takes it (seat order relative to the discarder). Standard
Zung Jung uses classical single-winner interception, not multi-win on
one discard.
