# Triplet

Three **identical** tiles (same design).

:::center
{333p} · {EEE} · {CCC}
:::

[Winds](rules-winds) and [dragons](rules-dragons) only form triplets (or
kongs) of the **same** design — not a “set of all winds” or “set of all
dragons.” Holding one of each wind ({E}{S}{W}{N}) or one of each dragon
({P}{F}{C}) is just three or four different tiles, not a set. You need
three Easts for an East triplet, three Reds for a Red dragon triplet,
and so on.

(Scoring patterns such as [Big Four Winds (3.3.4)](pattern-3.3.4) still
require real wind **triplets** — collecting one of each is not enough.)

Claiming the third copy from any player's discard is a **pung**. See
[Claims](rules-claims).

Four identical tiles declared as a set are a [kong](rules-kong-types),
not a longer triplet.
