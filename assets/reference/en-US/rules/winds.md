# Winds

Four **[honor](rules-honors)** designs — not a numbered [suit](rules-suits):

:::center
{E}{S}{W}{N}
:::

- **East** (東)
- **South** (南)
- **West** (西)
- **North** (北)

Winds have no rank order. They cannot form [sequences](rules-sequence) —
only [triplets](rules-triplet) or [kongs](rules-kong-types) of the
**same** design (three Easts, not “one of each wind”).

## Seat wind vs tile

Each player has a **[seat wind](rules-seats)** matching their seat. A
triplet or kong of **your** seat wind scores
[Value Honor (3.1)](pattern-3.1). Other players' seat winds do not score
Value Honor for you.

Zung Jung has **no** prevailing (round/table) wind.
