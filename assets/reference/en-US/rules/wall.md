# Wall & last tiles

Tiles are drawn from the **wall**. Standard Zung Jung keeps a
**dead wall** unused so the game ends cleanly.

## Dead wall

The last **14** wall tiles are the **dead wall** — not drawn as ordinary
turns. Supplements after kong still come from the wall arrangement used
for that purpose; the important beginner fact is: ordinary draws stop
before those reserved tiles.

In this app the dead wall is **not shown** on the table; only the live
wall is drawn.

## Seabed

The **seabed** is the **last live** wall tile — the last tile that can
be drawn as a normal (or supplement) draw before the wall is exhausted
for play. Self-draw on that tile is [Final Draw (9.1.1)](pattern-9.1.1).

If that draw is a kong supplement on the seabed, [Win on Kong
(9.2)](pattern-9.2) can stack with Final Draw.

## Riverbed

After the seabed is drawn, that player's **discard** (if no one has won
yet) is the **riverbed**. Winning on that discard is
[Final Discard (9.1.2)](pattern-9.1.2).

## Draw (no-win)

If the live wall is exhausted without a win — typically after the
seabed player discards and no one claims the riverbed — the hand is a
**draw** (no-win). No one scores a winning hand.
