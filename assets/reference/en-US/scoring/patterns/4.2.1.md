# Two Concealed Triplets

**4.2.1** · 二暗刻 · [hl]**5 points**[/]

Exactly **two** concealed triplets or concealed kongs.

**[Concealed](rules-concealed-open)** means all three tiles came from the
wall (including a [self-draw](rules-winning) win tile). Winning on a
discard of the completing tile makes that triplet **exposed**.

[muted]Series 4.2 — lower level of the concealed-triplet ladder.[/]
