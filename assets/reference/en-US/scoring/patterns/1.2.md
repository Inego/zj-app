# Concealed Hand

**1.2** · 門前清 · [hl]**5 points**[/]

[Regular hand](rules-hands) with **no [exposed melds](rules-concealed-open)**
before the win. Winning on a discard is allowed. A
**[concealed kong](rules-kong-types)** does not break this pattern; an
open kong does.

Irregular hands ([category 10](pattern-10)) **do not** count Concealed Hand.
