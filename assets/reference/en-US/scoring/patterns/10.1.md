# Thirteen Terminals

**10.1** · 十三么九 · [hl]**160 points**[/]

Among the 13 terminal and honor types: one pair plus one each
of the other twelve. Does **not** count [Concealed Hand (1.2)](pattern-1.2).

No special privileges: cannot rob a [concealed kong](rules-kong-types);
can be [intercepted](rules-winning) like a normal win.
